# Privacy Policy for MiniCount

**Last Updated: [Current Date]**

## Introduction

MiniCount ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Event Data**: Event titles, descriptions, dates, categories, and photos you add
- **Settings**: Your app preferences and configurations

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version
- **Usage Data**: App features used, interaction patterns
- **Crash Reports**: Technical data when app crashes occur

### Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services that may collect information:
- **Google Play Services**: For app functionality and analytics
- **AdMob**: For serving advertisements (free version only)

## How We Use Your Information

- **App Functionality**: To display your events and widgets
- **Improve Experience**: To understand how you use the app
- **Technical Support**: To help resolve issues
- **Analytics**: To improve app performance and features

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: All event data is stored locally on your device
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not upload your personal event data to our servers
- **Photo Access**: Photos you select remain on your device

## Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:
- **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly agree
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law
- **Service Providers**: Google Play Services and AdMob (as described in their privacy policies)

## Third-Party Services Privacy Policies

- Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## Advertising

The free version includes ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:
- Device identifiers
- Location data (approximate)
- Usage data

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Premium users do not see ads.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all data we store about you
- **Delete**: Delete your data by uninstalling the app
- **Opt-Out**: Disable analytics in app settings
- **Ad Preferences**: Manage ad personalization in device settings

## Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:
- Local encryption for sensitive data
- Secure communication protocols
- Regular security updates

## Children's Privacy

MiniCount is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Posting an in-app notice
- Requiring acceptance of new terms

## Data Retention

- **Event Data**: Retained locally until you delete or uninstall
- **Analytics**: Retained according to Google Analytics retention policies
- **Crash Reports**: Retained for 90 days

## International Users

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.
